# Font attribution

The application bundles **Inter Variable**, by Rasmus Andersson, at `InterVariable.woff2`. It is served locally; the app does not fetch a font from an external CDN at runtime.

- Project: [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) and [source repository](https://github.com/rsms/inter).
- Downloaded asset: [InterVariable.woff2](https://rsms.me/inter/font-files/InterVariable.woff2), obtained on 10 October 2026.
- Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete licence is retained in [OFL.txt](OFL.txt).

Preserve the licence when redistributing the font. The UI's typography references do not transfer another brand's identity or proprietary assets.

The opt-in olive branding preview bundles **Lobster Regular** by Pablo Impallari (Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1). [Source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lobster), [retained licence](Lobster-OFL.txt). This is a lettering approximation for owner review, not a verified identification of the sign's font. Inter remains the operational typeface.
